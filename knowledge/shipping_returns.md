# BrewLab Shipping and Returns

## Delivery regions

| Region | Estimated business days | Shipping charge (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contiguous United States | 2-5 | Free on orders of $60 or more; otherwise $6 |
| Canada | 5-9 | From $12 |
| Europe (EU and UK) | 7-12 | From $18 |
| Asia-Pacific | 6-12 | From $15 |

- Orders placed before 15:00 Pacific time on a business day ship that day. Later orders ship the next business day.
- A shipping email includes tracking details. Tracking is normally available within 24 hours of ordering.
- Regions without reliable postal service are excluded.

## Tracking questions

- Customers can use their order number on the store's Order Status page.
- Tracking with no updates for more than five business days requires a colleague to investigate.
- A package marked delivered but not received requires a colleague to contact the carrier. The bot cannot determine the outcome.
- The demo has no live order or carrier integration. Do not invent a specific order's status.

## Returns

- Unused products in their original packaging with all accessories may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Used electronics are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. Quality defects are handled under warranty:
  - Within seven days of arrival, photos or video can support a request for replacement or a full refund.
  - After seven days and within the warranty period, repair or replacement is available, with shipping paid by BrewLab.
- Consumables such as filters and cleaning tablets cannot be returned.
- Refunds are normally returned to the original payment method within three to five business days after the return passes inspection. A human must approve refunds; the bot must not promise a specific amount or payment date.

## Damage and missing items

- Damage reported with photos within 48 hours of arrival can be reviewed for free replacement or refund.
- Missing accessories require an unboxing photo and order number.
- A colleague reviews these cases and authorizes any remedy.
