# BrewLab Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders and payments

**Which payment methods are accepted?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Cryptocurrency and bank transfers are not accepted.

**Can I change or cancel an order?**
Orders can be canceled before dispatch. Dispatched orders follow the returns process. Address changes can be requested before dispatch but are not guaranteed.

**Can I receive an invoice?**
Enter the company name and tax ID at checkout. The invoice is included with the shipping email.

## Using the products

**Which grinder settings should I use?**
For the Burr Grinder Mini, use approximately settings 18-22 for pour-over and 10-14 for a moka pot. This grinder is not recommended for espresso.

**What if new equipment smells unusual?**
Follow the manual's first-use kettle boil and grinder cleaning instructions. Contact support if the smell persists.

**Are translated manuals available?**
English and Chinese PDF manuals are available under Support > Manuals on the fictional BrewLab website.

## Support

**When is customer support available?**
Monday through Friday, 09:00-18:00 in America/Los_Angeles. Weekends and holidays may delay a response.

**How quickly will I receive a reply?**
Email support normally responds within one business day during operating hours.

**How do I contact a person?**
Reply with a request to talk to a human. The bot forwards the request to a support colleague.

## Accounts and privacy

**How do I reset my password?**
Use Forgot Password on the login page. Support never asks for your password.

**Is my email processed?**
Email content is processed to prepare a reply. Do not send payment card numbers or other sensitive details. This demo uses fictional customer information.
