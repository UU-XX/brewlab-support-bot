# BrewLab Pricing and Discounts

## List prices

See the price table in `products.md`. All prices are in USD, excluding taxes and shipping.

## Batch quotes

| Quantity in one order | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| 1-4 units | None |
| 5-9 units | 10% |
| 10-24 units | 15% |
| 25 or more units | Request a custom quote from a colleague |

- Bulk discounts apply to the same SKU or to items in a single order.
- Orders of more than 10 units can request custom gift boxes and branded cards, adding five business days.
- Bulk orders require a 50% deposit.

## Promotions

- New customers receive 10% off their first order with code `BREW10`.
- Promotion codes cannot be combined with bulk discounts.
- Email subscribers receive quarterly offers; the applicable discount is specified in the offer email.

## Price comparisons

- BrewLab does not promise the lowest price or guarantee third-party prices.
- Customers may submit a screenshot of a lower price at an authorized seller before placing an order. A colleague must verify the offer and decide whether to match it.
- Price changes do not apply retroactively. Existing orders retain their original price.

## Taxes

- US sales tax depends on the delivery address.
- International recipients are responsible for applicable import duties and taxes.
