## Compatibility and specifications

- **Burr Grinder Mini:** stainless steel burrs, not ceramic. Suitable for pour-over and moka pots; not recommended for high-pressure espresso because the grind is too coarse.
- **Espresso Press Pro:** compatible with 58 mm baskets and portafilters, including BL-CLP-06. Does not support capsules.
- **Gooseneck Kettle 1.0L:** rated for 110 V / 60 Hz North American power. A suitable transformer is needed for European mains power.
- **BrewLab Scale v2:** IPX3 splash resistance; do not submerge.

## Package contents

- Pour-Over Starter Kit: one dripper, one 600 ml server, 100 filters, one digital scale and one instruction card.
- Espresso Press Pro: one unit, two baskets (single and double), one tamper and one cleaning brush.

## Warranty

- Electronic products (kettles and scales): 24 months from purchase.
- Non-electronic products (drippers, portafilters and grinders): 12 months from purchase.
- Accidental damage, unauthorized disassembly and consumables such as filters are excluded.
- Warranty eligibility and remedies require a human support agent.
